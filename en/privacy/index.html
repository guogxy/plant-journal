Scope and contact

This policy covers the Plant Journal app for iPhone and iPad and its official website. The app is currently in preparation. Questions about privacy or a request concerning information you sent us can be emailed to gggxxxyyyy@gmail.com.

Plant Journal does not require an app account. It contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. We do not operate a separate photo-upload server.

Information on your device

The app stores the content you add or create: plant names and settings, photos and their available dates or metadata, reference guides, notes, care history and schedules, movie settings, generated videos, and backup information. It also stores preferences and the identifiers needed to manage local records and optional cloud snapshots.

Original image files are retained without stripping their embedded metadata. The app reads the capture date and does not request location permission, but an imported original can still contain camera, device, date, or geographic EXIF metadata. That metadata can remain with originals included in exports or iCloud backups. Review the source files before sharing.

Image analysis, alignment assistance, correction, highlight suggestions, and optional AI interpolation run on the device. The bundled interpolation model does not require a runtime download or a third-party AI API. Intermediate images may be generated and should not be treated as observed plant changes.

The app may check available storage and keep working files to complete previews, exports, or backups. Removing a plant or photo from the app does not remove copies already exported to Photos, Files, another app, a system device backup, or an existing cloud snapshot.

Permissions you choose

  • Camera: used to take photos when you start a capture.
  • Photos: used when you select photos to import or choose to save an exported result. System permissions and the photo picker limit access as provided by iOS.
  • Notifications: used for local photo and care reminders. Permission, Focus settings, and system scheduling can affect delivery.
  • Calendar and Reminders: used when you explicitly export a plan or task. Permission is requested for the relevant feature. These are separate copies managed by those apps; changing or deleting an item in one place may not update the other.

You can review permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission can make its related feature unavailable; other available features can still be used.

Optional private iCloud backup

iCloud backup is off by default. If you enable it and a usable iCloud account and connection are available, the app uploads backup snapshots and associated media to its private CloudKit database for your Apple Account. This includes photos, plant records, notes, care information, movie recipes and generated media included in the archive, as well as a device identifier, device name, and backup time used to distinguish snapshots.

This uses network access and your iCloud storage. Apple processes iCloud service and account information under its own policies. The app’s automatic backup runs while the app is active in the foreground; completion depends on network access, available space, account status, and service availability. It is backup and manual recovery, not continuous two-way synchronization.

Normally the app keeps the latest three complete snapshots per device when cleanup succeeds. A failed cleanup can leave more snapshots. Older shared media that is no longer referenced is not automatically reclaimed, to avoid deleting files another device may still use; it may continue to occupy your iCloud storage until you explicitly delete the app’s cloud backups.

Turning backup off stops future uploads; it does not delete earlier cloud copies. The app’s Delete All Cloud Backups action deletes this app’s cloud snapshots and media across devices and disables automatic backup on the current device. Turn backup off on your other devices first, or they may upload again. Check that deletion succeeds. Deletion and retention within Apple’s infrastructure are also governed by Apple’s service policies.

Restoring a selected snapshot creates independent local copies instead of merging with existing records. iOS system device backup is separate from this optional in-app feature. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Exports and other services

You choose whether to export a film, save a local backup file, or send content through the system share sheet. Photos, videos, and archives can contain plant names, notes, dates, image metadata, or other personal content you included. Review the content and destination before sharing.

When you choose a cloud drive, Photos service, calendar account, email provider, or another app, that service can store or transmit the copy under its own settings and policies. Plant Journal cannot recall a copy already shared or delete it from someone else’s device.

Retention, deletion, and support

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app’s local data. Uninstalling can remove the local archive; do not rely on uninstalling to remove exported copies or iCloud snapshots. Keep a verified backup of records you want to retain.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and the attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate the request, retain them only as needed for support and legitimate legal or security purposes, and do not use them for advertising. Do not send a full plant archive or sensitive photos unless necessary. You can request deletion of support correspondence through the contact above, subject to applicable retention requirements.

We cannot retrieve or delete records stored only on your device or in your private iCloud database for you; use the app and Apple’s account and storage controls. Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to information we hold about you. Contact support to exercise them.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, third-party fonts, embedded social widgets, contact forms, or our own cookies. There is no app-content upload feature on this website.

GitHub may process basic request information such as IP address, browser/device details, requested pages, and access times to provide and secure its hosting service. Its handling is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. External links, including email links, are handled by the service you choose.

Policy changes

We will update this page when the app or its data handling changes and revise the date above. When a change requires a new permission or consent, the relevant app or system flow will request it. The current policy remains available from the app and this website.
