Start with a useful reference

Create a plant record, then take or import a first photo. Choose an uncluttered background and leave extra space around the plant for future growth and possible stabilization. Confirm the assisted outline rather than assuming every edge is correct.

If the outline is incomplete, improve contrast against the background, try another image, or use the translucent reference and adjustable pot guide. Repeated leaf shapes, shadows, and overlapping leaves can make automatic extraction difficult. Anchor your composition to the pot or another stable part of the scene.

Choose a capture plan

Choose the subject and recording period, or work backward from the finished film length. The plan estimates the required photo count and interval. Use a short trial before a continuous session to check framing, available storage, lighting flicker, and processing time.

For steady artificial lighting, use supported exposure, focus, and white-balance locks after checking the scene. For changing natural light or day-to-night capture, avoid a lock that makes later frames unusably dark or bright. Device capabilities vary. Keep continuous interval capture in the foreground, with the device stable, powered as needed, and able to stay cool.

Photo and care reminders

Use the linked photo reminder from your shooting plan, or add care tasks such as watering, fertilizing, pruning, and treatments. The dates and recurrence should match the actual needs of your plant; a reminder is not a care prescription. Record completion so later reviews have context.

If reminders do not appear, check the app’s permission in Settings → Notifications, Focus settings, the reminder’s next date, and the device time zone. If you exported to Calendar or Reminders, also check that destination’s account and permissions. Export creates a separate copy; changing one app does not guarantee the other is updated.

Make and check the film

Select your photos, choose the timing, and make a preview first. Start with original frames, then compare exposure smoothing, color alignment, plant color protection, and stabilization as needed. Select a stable reference region for deflicker; moving leaves or changing shadows can make that region unsuitable. Check the stabilization safe border to protect leaf tips.

AI interpolation is off by default. It can smooth a transition but may invent or distort detail, particularly across days. Try it on a short section and review the generated frames. Lower preview resolution does not mean the original photos were changed. On a long or hot-running export, reduce the selected section or output size, allow the device to cool, and try again. Keep enough free space for working files and the result.

Back up and restore locally

Use the app’s local backup tool to save a .plantbackup archive to a destination you control. The app checks file integrity during recovery and shows a restore summary. Keep the exported archive intact and keep another copy somewhere safe if the records matter to you.

Recovery makes new independent local records; it does not overwrite existing plants or combine edits. If an archive cannot be opened, keep the original unchanged, check that it has finished downloading from your file provider, and retry with enough free space. Do not delete the source records until you have checked the restored copy.

Understand optional iCloud backup

iCloud backup starts off. Enable it in the app only when you want photos and records uploaded to your private iCloud storage. Check the successful-backup time and available snapshots while the app is in the foreground. If it is unavailable, verify your Apple Account, iCloud permissions and storage, connection, and that you are using a correctly configured device build.

This is not live syncing: restore a chosen snapshot manually, creating independent copies. Turning backup off keeps existing cloud copies. Normally three complete snapshots are retained per device, but cleanup failures and old shared media can use more space. To remove the app’s cloud archive, turn backup off on other devices, use Delete All Cloud Backups, and verify that it succeeds. Local and already-exported copies are separate.
