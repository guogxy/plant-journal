Plans and care reminders

Interval, frame-count, finished-length, and storage suggestions are estimates based on the settings and records available. Actual plant movement, camera timing, device processing, free space, and lighting can differ. Check the plan and test the setup before relying on a long session.

Care reminders and logs organize information you enter. They do not identify a disease, establish that a treatment worked, or provide pesticide, fertilizer, toxicity, or professional horticultural advice. Follow the product label and appropriate professional guidance for any treatment. Do not infer a health assessment or a precise growth measurement from counts or highlight suggestions.

Capture, reminders, and exports

Continuous interval capture requires the app to stay in the foreground. Locking the device, moving to another app, low power, heat, insufficient storage, or an interruption may stop the session. Local notification delivery depends on permissions and iOS settings and is not guaranteed at an exact time.

Calendar and Reminders exports create separate items. Review the destination, time zone, repeat rules, and permissions; later edits or deletions may not be reflected back in Plant Journal. Review a movie preview and its crop before exporting, particularly where new leaves approach the edge.

Correction and AI-generated images

Exposure correction, color alignment, stabilization, crop, and speed changes alter the presentation of your sequence. They may reduce some artifacts and introduce others. Optional AI interpolation generates intermediate images rather than recovering a moment that was photographed. It can distort fine detail, moving leaves, occlusion, and transitions across long gaps.

Original photos remain the source record. Review generated sections and keep the available AI labels when representing the result to others. Do not use an AI-enhanced film as scientific, evidentiary, or diagnostic proof of an actual transition.

Storage and backups

You are responsible for keeping copies of records you want to retain and checking a backup after export. Local recovery creates an independent copy; it does not merge changes across devices. Optional iCloud backup is off by default, uses your Apple Account’s storage, requires network/service availability, and is not real-time syncing.

Turning cloud backup off does not delete previous snapshots. Deleting all app cloud backups affects the app’s cloud copies across your devices; another device with backup still enabled may upload again. Exported files and system device backups are separate.
